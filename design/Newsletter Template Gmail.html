Acme Signal

Acme Signal closed its Series A this month — $12M led by a top-tier growth fund. Founder-focus in practice: our Support Partner ran diligence data-room prep so the team could stay heads-down on customers through the raise.

Across the portfolio

Market insight

Seed rounds are getting smaller — and that's good for our model.

AI-native teams are reaching Series A milestones on half the headcount of two years ago. Leaner rounds mean less dilution at entry and more room for disciplined co-investors. We're seeing the strongest opportunities in oversubscribed rounds where operational support — not more capital — is the constraint.

Deal flow

We reviewed 87 companies this month and took 12 first calls. Two are in diligence: an AI compliance platform for logistics and a vertical agent for insurance claims. Both rounds are forming around top-tier leads — consistent with our co-invest discipline. We pass on anything where we can't add operational leverage.
