# The Support Partner model

## Operational drag should never be your bottleneck.

Every shuckerVC portfolio company gets a full-time operator: the Support Partner. They run finance, hiring, vendors, and reporting—anything that pulls your attention away from your product and customers.

Finance ops — 2h /week of founder time. Books closed by the second week of the month — start to finish, on just two hours of your time a week.

Recruiting — 8h /week of founder time. Four engineers hired end to end — sourced, screened, and closed — on eight hours of your time a week.

GTM — 100% qualified no wasted meetings. Every sales call you take is a qualified lead — pipeline built and screened before it reaches you.

PR — 12h on podcasts earned media. Your Support Partner handles the media — right shows, right audiences — so you just show up, and your reach with customers and investors keeps compounding.

### One hire. The depth of a firm.

You make one hire — a Support Partner, salary at cost. Behind them sits the firm: GPs who hire, train, and manage them, and a specialist bench on call when the work needs depth.

### Your Support Partner

We source and vet the candidates — you pick whoever best fits your stage. Your Support Partner is on our W2, embedded full-time in your company, running finance, hiring, vendors, and reporting day to day.

### shuckerVC GPs

Behind every Support Partner sit the fund's GPs — bringing their operating experience and the network of Support Partners across the portfolio. They hire, train, and manage yours — you never manage the manager.

### Specialist network

On-call specialists in finance, people, growth, and security — pulled in when the work needs more depth than one operator.
